# How to make certain organizations independent

UPSC chief officer appointment process:
  - creation of anonymized election files for the candidates
  - files include a thorough personality analysis as well
  - files are assigned a number, and a copy given to respective IAS in sealed cover and email, 
    so that the officer can prove ownership of anonymous file if required
  - all IAS, current and retired, vote for the anonymous candidates
  - winning file gets processed and all candidates are revealed
  - winning candidate appointed directly by president, just like the PM
  - legislature is not involved in "recommendations" of any kind


Election Commission:
  - every promotion in the top 3 ranks must be done by election:
    - exam by UPSC, via Testing Agency
    - interview by UPSC
    - candidate shorlisted by UPSC
    - election where list of voters = 
      all employees of election commission of same rank +
      all employees of election commission of lower rank
  - appointment is done via UPSC, directly under president, just like the PM
  - legislature is not involved in "recommendations" of any kind


Judiciary:
  - appointment is done via UPSC
  - ANY lawyer wanting to become judge applies to UPSC,
    if they fulfill the basic criteria
  - exam by UPSC, via Testing Agency as required by holder of public office
  - interview by UPSC, as required by holder of public office
  - candidate shortlist prepared by UPSC
  - interview by collegium of the court + 
    randomly selected (x) chief justices of other courts of same rank +
    1 chief justic of higher rank;
    where (x) is the number of members in the collegium
  - in case of supreme court judge
    - president as leader of executive
    - prime minister as leader of legislature
    will also be part of interviews
